Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Ultra Medical Center collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use our website, , appointment services, report request services, live chat, contact forms or related digital services.

We treat patient information as confidential and use it only for healthcare service delivery, administrative support, patient communication, safety, compliance and service improvement.

This website supports patient service requests and general healthcare information. It is not a replacement for emergency medical care. In an emergency, please contact emergency services or visit the nearest emergency department.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal identification details such as name, date of birth, nationality, Emirates ID, passport details or medical record number.
  • Contact details such as mobile number, email address and UAE address.
  • Appointment details such as department, doctor, preferred date, visit type and reason for visit.
  • Insurance details such as provider, policy number, network, expiry date and eligibility information.
  • Medical service request information such as report type, medical records request, uploaded authorization documents or request notes.
  • Website interaction information such as pages visited, forms submitted, chat requests and device/browser details.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information for legitimate healthcare, operational and patient support purposes, including:

  • Scheduling, confirming, rescheduling and managing patient appointments.
  • Creating or updating patient registration information.
  • Verifying patient identity before releasing reports or medical records.
  • Responding to enquiries submitted through contact forms, live chat, WhatsApp or telephone.
  • Processing insurance eligibility checks, pre-approval support and billing-related requests.
  • Providing access, visit history, report status, medication information and appointment details.
  • Improving website usability, patient service quality and digital healthcare workflows.

4. Medical Records and Reports

Medical records, lab reports, radiology reports, prescriptions, visit summaries and related clinical documents are handled as sensitive health information.

  • Records are released only after appropriate identity or authorization verification.
  • Medical records requests may require Emirates ID, passport copy, signed authorization or additional verification.
  • Some reports may require doctor review before release.
  • Records may be shared through secure email, access or controlled clinic collection based on request type and verification status.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell patient information. Information may be shared only where necessary for healthcare service delivery, patient support, insurance processing, billing, legal obligations or authorized patient requests.

  • Doctors, nurses and authorized care team members involved in patient care.
  • Insurance providers or third-party administrators for eligibility, approval and claims support.
  • Laboratory, radiology or medical service partners involved in diagnostic or treatment services.
  • Regulatory or legal authorities when required by applicable law or official request.
  • Authorized representatives when valid consent or legal authorization is provided.

7. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, improve user experience, remember preferences and understand how visitors use our pages.

  • Analytics information may help us improve page design, navigation and patient services.
  • You may manage cookies through your browser settings, but some features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You may contact Ultra Medical Center to request support regarding your personal information, patient record access, correction of registration details, report collection, medical records release or privacy-related concerns.

  • Request access to available personal or medical information subject to verification.
  • Request correction of inaccurate contact or registration information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used in patient services.
  • Withdraw optional communication preferences where applicable.
  • Report suspected unauthorized access or privacy concerns.

9. Data Retention and Security

We retain patient and service information as required for healthcare delivery, administrative records, legal obligations and continuity of care. Retention periods may vary depending on the information type, medical record requirements and applicable regulations.

We use administrative, technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No digital system is completely risk-free, but we take reasonable steps to protect patient information.
